Precise censor models (NudeNet)
===============================

Small model (Settings -> Censor accuracy -> "Precise - nudity detector")
  Nothing to do: the page downloads it automatically (about 12 MB) and your browser caches it.
  To keep a local copy instead, save it here as:  nudenet-320n.onnx
  https://github.com/notAI-tech/NudeNet/releases/download/v3.4-weights/320n.onnx

Large model (Settings -> Censor accuracy -> "Precise, large")
  More accurate, about 100 MB. Download it once and save it in THIS folder as:  nudenet-640m.onnx
  https://github.com/notAI-tech/NudeNet/releases/download/v3.4-weights/640m.onnx
  Turn on "Use graphics card" for this one.
